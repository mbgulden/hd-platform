Family Test Guide

A simple walkthrough for testing the signup, Telegram onboarding, and first Human Design bot experience in staging.

Staging test
Privacy note: This is a family/beta test. Michael and the support agent may review operational status and, only with your consent, conversation summaries/transcripts to improve the bot. Do not share private details you do not want reviewed during testing.

1. What you need

  • A Telegram account installed on your phone or desktop.
  • Your regular email address, or a test-friendly email you can receive messages at.
  • About 10-15 minutes.

2. Sign up through Stripe test checkout

  1. Open the staging signup page Michael sends you. Usually this is https://staging.humandesignengine.com/deconditioning/.
  2. Choose the test offer Michael asks you to try.
  3. Enter your email address.
  4. Stripe will open in test mode. Use the card below.
Stripe test fieldUse this value
Card number4242 4242 4242 4242
ExpirationAny future date, e.g. 12/34
CVCAny 3 digits, e.g. 123
ZIP/postalAny valid ZIP, e.g. 83702

3. Open your Telegram Sanctuary

  1. After checkout, the success page should show one clear button: Open Telegram.
  2. Tap it. Telegram should open the Human Design Companion bot.
  3. Press Start if Telegram asks.
  4. The bot will ask what simple name the space should answer to. Try Ember, Mira, George, or a short custom name.
  5. Wait for the bot to say the space is open. Cold setup can take a minute or two. If it says contact support, tell Michael exactly what you saw.

4. Test the first conversation

Use normal human language. The point is not to be polite to the software. Try a real question or one of these prompts:

Gentle start
“I’m here. What can you help me with?”
Real life
“I keep getting stuck in the same pattern with work/family. What should I look at?”
Chart request
“Can you help me build my Human Design chart?”
Memory check
“Can you remember what we talked about if I come back later?”

5. What to watch for

  • Signup felt simple and one-click.
  • The Telegram link opened the right bot.
  • The guide-name choice felt natural.
  • The bot asked one question at a time.
  • The bot did not overwhelm you with a giant wall of text.
  • The bot did not immediately demand birth data unless you asked for a chart.
  • The bot sounded like a grounded Human Design practice room, not a fake friend or generic chatbot.
  • If you asked for a chart, it handled missing birth time gracefully.

6. Send feedback to Michael

Please send Michael:

  • What worked smoothly.
  • Where you got stuck.
  • Any exact bot message that felt weird, too long, fake, confusing, or surprisingly good.
  • Your device: iPhone, Android, desktop Telegram, etc.
